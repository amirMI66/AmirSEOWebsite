What I measure

Speed is a ranking signal. I check yours against Google's thresholds.

Core Web Vitals measure how fast and stable your pages feel to real visitors. These are the targets every audit tests.

Common questions

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes can show up within a few weeks. Rankings for competitive keywords usually build over three to six months. I'll give you a realistic timeline after the audit.

Do you guarantee a #1 ranking?

No. Nobody controls Google's results, and anyone who guarantees a position is guessing. I guarantee clear work, honest reporting and a plan based on your data.

Do I need to change my website platform?

Almost never. I work with WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and custom sites.

What does the free audit include?

A quick review of your site's top issues and biggest opportunities, delivered on a short call. There's no obligation to continue.
