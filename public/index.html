FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does SEO consulting include?

SEO consulting can include technical SEO, content optimization, keyword and search-intent research, local SEO, site architecture, internal linking, SEO strategy, and AI/LLM visibility. The exact scope depends on your website and goals.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. You can start with a one-time audit or choose a monthly subscription for ongoing SEO support. Monthly plans are designed to stay flexible, so you can adjust or cancel as your needs change.

How long does SEO take to generate results?

SEO is a long-term growth channel, and timelines vary depending on your website, industry, competition, technical condition, and starting point. Some improvements can have an impact relatively quickly, while larger organic growth typically takes several months.

What do I receive each week?

You’ll have access to a live SEO dashboard and receive clear weekly updates on performance, progress, and priorities. You’ll see what changed, how organic traffic and leads are trending, what’s been worked on, and what’s coming next—giving you complete visibility into your SEO investment.

How do you measure SEO success?

Rankings are only one part of the picture. I focus on business outcomes such as qualified organic traffic, leads, conversions, revenue, and visibility across traditional and AI-powered search.

What makes your approach different?

I focus on the SEO work that can have a measurable business impact—not chasing rankings for the sake of rankings. Every engagement is prioritized around your goals, with clear recommendations, transparent reporting, and a focus on turning organic visibility into qualified leads and revenue.
